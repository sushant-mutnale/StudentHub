# Report limitations use structured categories plus a short explanation

Report limitations use fixed categories — insufficient evidence, conflicting evidence, time/coverage constraint, mode/technical limitation, hint assistance — each with a short factual explanation. They describe the evidence and session, never the candidate's character or intent.

**Status:** accepted — limitation model. Exact wording rules and per-category thresholds remain open.

## Consequences

- Limitations are stored as a category plus explanation, not free-form prose alone.
- The category list is versioned with the rubric/report schema.
- Explanations must be factual and non-accusatory; character judgements are invalid.
- A low-confidence score must identify at least one limitation category where applicable.
- Limitations do not change scores by themselves; they explain the evidence.
- Categories must be localizable without changing meaning.
