# Responsible baseline: disclosure, accessibility and English-first delivery

The first tier provides prominent AI disclosure and limits at session start and in the report; a text alternative to voice; keyboard and screen-reader support to WCAG 2.2 AA for core interview/report flows; and English-first content with externalized strings.

**Status:** accepted — disclosure/accessibility baseline. Disclosure copy, audit method, accommodation policy and localization roadmap remain open.

## Consequences

- The candidate is told they are interacting with AI, how scoring works, and what the system cannot do.
- Every voice flow has a complete text alternative; voice is never the only path.
- Core interview and report flows are tested against WCAG 2.2 AA, not assumed accessible.
- Strings are externalized so localization is possible without a rewrite.
- Additional languages and accommodations are not promised at launch.
- Report caveats and limitations must match the disclosure shown before the session.
