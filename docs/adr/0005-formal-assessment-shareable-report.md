# Formal assessments produce candidate-owned shareable reports

Formal assessments are timed, controlled and scored against a defined competency blueprint, and they produce a structured report that the candidate owns and may deliberately share with a recruiter or placement cell. The first production milestone has no live proctoring, identity verification, or human reviewer sign-off; those remain open for a later integrity tier. Sharing is explicit, scoped, revocable and time-limited, and practice evidence never alters formal scores.

**Status:** accepted — first production integrity level. Higher-integrity tiers remain open.

## Consequences

- The report must be portable and versioned, with rubric, evidence excerpts and session-integrity metadata (time bounds, hints disabled, blueprint version).
- Recruiter/placement access is read-only and consent-controlled; shared copies must expire and be revocable.
- The system must not claim identity verification or proctoring it does not perform.
- Candidate corrections are limited to factual annotations/disputes, not silent score edits, unless later decided otherwise.
- Deletion and retention must cover shared copies and derived report artifacts.
- Proctoring, identity verification, human review and appeals are explicitly deferred, not silently omitted.
