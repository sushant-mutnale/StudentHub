# Initial per-type weight profiles

Core-competency weights (problem-solving / technical depth / communication / structure / evidence):

| Type | Problem-solving | Technical depth | Communication | Structure | Evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Behavioral/HR | 15 | 10 | 30 | 25 | 20 |
| Technical knowledge | 25 | 35 | 15 | 10 | 15 |
| Resume/project | 15 | 25 | 20 | 10 | 30 |
| DSA/coding | 35 | 25 | 10 | 10 | 20 |

Type-specific dimensions (collaboration, ownership/impact, code quality) are scored separately and are not part of these five weights.

**Status:** accepted — initial weights. Values are subject to calibration; changes require a new rubric version.

## Consequences

- Weights are stored with the rubric version and shown in the report.
- Equal weighting is not used; each type emphasizes what it is designed to reveal.
- Type-specific dimensions do not silently alter core weights.
- Calibration can change values only through a new version; historical reports are not recomputed.
- A weight change invalidates direct comparison with earlier sessions of the same type.
- The report must explain what the weights mean without implying precision beyond the evidence.
