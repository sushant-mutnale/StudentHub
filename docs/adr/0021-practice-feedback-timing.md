# Practice offers on-demand hints but no live scores

Practice sessions offer candidate-initiated, bounded hints and no live scores; the full report arrives at the end. Formal assessments offer neither hints nor live scores.

**Status:** accepted — feedback timing and hint policy. Content bounds and confidence treatment remain open.

## Consequences

- The hint action is explicit, logged, and unavailable in formal assessments.
- No per-answer score is shown during practice, so the candidate cannot optimize to an evaluator mid-session.
- Hint usage becomes part of the session record and may be reflected in confidence/limitations.
- The end report must be generated from the full session, not a running score.
- The interviewer must not leak evaluative language ("that's a strong answer") during practice.
- Formal assessments require the hint path to be disabled server-side, not merely hidden in the UI.
