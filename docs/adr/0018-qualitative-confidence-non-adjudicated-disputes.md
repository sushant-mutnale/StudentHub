# Qualitative confidence bands and non-adjudicated disputes

Each competency score carries a low/medium/high confidence band with the reasons and limitations that produced it. A candidate dispute creates an annotation and a visible unresolved flag; it does not change the score, trigger an automatic re-score, or imply human adjudication.

**Status:** accepted — confidence bands and dispute model. Exact per-competency thresholds and limitation wording remain open.

## Consequences

- Confidence is stored as a band plus structured reasons, not a percentage.
- Consumers see the band and limitations alongside the score; the band is not decorative.
- Disputes have a status (open/resolved-as-annotated) but do not mutate the score.
- No human review queue is implied or promised in the first tier.
- Repeated disputes must not create a re-scoring loop or a gaming channel.
- Reports must state that an unresolved flag is not a finding of error.
